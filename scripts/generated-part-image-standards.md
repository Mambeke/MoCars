# MoCars generated part-gallery standards

## Scope

- Final catalogue target: 100 parts — 50 New and 50 Used.
- The four existing listings count toward the target; 96 additional listings are planned.
- Every listing must have exactly two standalone photographs: `front.jpg` and `rear.jpg`.
- Publish a listing only after both images have been reviewed and accepted.

## Image requirements

- Photorealistic 16:9 product photography at 1376×768.
- The two views must show the same physical product with matching geometry, materials, fittings, colour and condition.
- `front.jpg`: complete primary three-quarter product view.
- `rear.jpg`: opposite three-quarter view showing the reverse side, connectors, mounting points or construction details.
- Neutral light-grey studio surface, soft diffused lighting, natural contact shadow and realistic scale.
- New parts must look unused and clean, without unrealistic perfection or plastic-looking CGI materials.
- Used parts must show restrained, credible wear appropriate to the component—never artificial damage, heavy corrosion or unsafe defects unless supplied as an actual stock fact.
- Keep the complete product comfortably in frame. Do not create grids, collages or split views.
- Do not add people, hands, tools, workshop clutter, packaging or unrelated objects unless specifically supplied.

## Accuracy and customer trust

- Never invent a brand, OEM number, serial number, engine code or certification mark, and never place fake text or unreadable pseudo-labels on a product image.
- Use the MoCars listing reference as the catalogue part number unless a verified OEM number is supplied.
- Every published part must have a numeric estimated USD price, formatted publicly as `US$ 120`.
- Every published part must have a full description covering its function, included components, condition, potential multi-vehicle applications and the measurements or specifications that determine fitment.
- Compatibility examples must be qualified as selected or potential applications; do not claim that one generic component fits every listed vehicle.
- Every description must state that the price is a rough estimate and end with:
  `Contact MoCars to confirm the actual part, condition, fitment and availability before ordering.`
- Use `Various manufacturers` when no verified manufacturer is supplied.
- Use `Available to order` for New parts and `Available` for Used parts unless verified stock information is supplied.
- Public part pages must not display “On request,” “Not supplied” or empty compatibility fields.
- Generated products must not be described as photographs of a specific physical stock unit.
- Used-part condition must be confirmed by MoCars before ordering.

## Review checklist

Before committing or publishing, verify:

1. Product type is immediately recognisable and mechanically plausible.
2. Both views depict the same product.
3. Connectors, fasteners, mounting points, vents, hoses and moving parts are coherent.
4. No fake logos, labels, writing or part numbers appear.
5. New/Used condition is visually appropriate.
6. No duplicated components, warped geometry, floating parts or impossible shadows.
7. Dimensions are exactly 1376×768.
8. Both files load successfully.
9. Listing contains no unsupported product facts.
10. The required MoCars confirmation line is present.
